Influencer Partnership Contract

Agreement Overview

This Influencer Partnership Contract ("Agreement") is entered into between the Influencer (hereinafter "Influencer") and Shayan Industry (hereinafter "Company"), effective upon the date of electronic acceptance by the Influencer.

1. Revenue Split

The revenue generated from the partnership activities shall be split as follows:

  • Influencer: Sixty percent (60%) of the net revenue.
  • Shayan Industry: Forty percent (40%) of the net revenue.

Net revenue is defined as gross revenue less any direct costs, chargebacks, or refunds directly attributable to the partnership activities.

2. Contract Duration

This Agreement shall commence on the date of acceptance and shall remain in full force and effect for a period of two (2) months ("Term"), unless terminated earlier as per the provisions of this Agreement.

3. Responsibilities of Parties

Both parties agree to uphold their respective responsibilities as outlined below:

  • Influencer Responsibilities:
    1. Actively manage and engage with their community across all designated platforms.
    2. Create and publish high-quality content promoting the Company's initiatives, products, or services as agreed upon.
    3. Maintain a positive and professional image, ensuring all content aligns with the Company's brand guidelines and values.
    4. Provide regular reports or updates on community engagement metrics as reasonably requested by the Company.
    5. Adhere to all applicable laws and regulations, including disclosure requirements for sponsored content.
  • Shayan Industry Responsibilities:
    1. Handle all marketing strategies and campaigns related to the partnership.
    2. Implement initiatives for community growth and expansion.
    3. Negotiate and finalize deals with exchanges and other relevant partners.
    4. Provide the Influencer with necessary marketing materials, product information, and support.
    5. Ensure timely and accurate calculation and distribution of the Influencer's revenue share.

4. Intellectual Property

All content created by the Influencer specifically for this partnership, including but not limited to videos, posts, images, and text, shall be jointly owned by both the Influencer and Shayan Industry for the duration of this contract and for promotional purposes thereafter. Neither party shall use the other's trademarks, logos, or copyrighted material without prior written consent, except as explicitly permitted by this Agreement.

5. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary or sensitive information disclosed during the course of this partnership, including but not limited to business strategies, financial data, and unreleased product information. This obligation of confidentiality shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

6. Termination

This Agreement may be terminated under the following conditions:

  • By mutual written agreement of both parties.
  • By either party, with fourteen (14) days written notice, if the other party breaches any material term of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.
  • Immediately by Shayan Industry if the Influencer engages in any conduct that significantly harms the Company's reputation or brand image.

7. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [Your Country/State, e.g., Pakistan]. Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach, termination, or invalidity thereof, shall first be attempted to be resolved through good faith negotiations between the parties. If a resolution cannot be reached, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration in [City, Country, e.g., Faisalabad, Pakistan] in accordance with the rules of [Arbitration Body, if applicable].

8. Breach of Contract and Legal Actions

In the event of a material breach of this Agreement by either party, the non-breaching party reserves the right to pursue all available legal remedies, including but not limited to:

  • Seeking monetary damages for losses incurred as a result of the breach.
  • Seeking injunctive relief to prevent further unauthorized actions or to compel performance.
  • Termination of this Agreement without further obligation to the breaching party.
  • Recovery of legal fees and costs incurred in enforcing the terms of this Agreement.

Any actions taken by the Influencer that are deemed to be in direct violation of the terms outlined herein, particularly those related to community management, content quality, brand representation, or confidentiality, may result in immediate termination of the contract and potential legal action for damages.
